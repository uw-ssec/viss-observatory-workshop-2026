## Event

- Schmidt Sciences Observatory System symposium at a villa outside Florence,
  Italy, **Nov 3-5 2026**, roughly 180-200 attendees.
- **Day 1 (Nov 3):** our tutorial, "Coding with AI Agents", runs as a
  parallel session of about **2 hours** (the original ISS version was 2.5-3
  hours, so material must be culled). It is the first formal course of the
  convening.
- **Day 2+:** observatory hackathon sessions in parallel rooms, all on the
  same JupyterHub with JupyterAI and agents, using Schmidt observatory data
  products (S3). Our tutorial must not duplicate those sessions.
- Audience is mixed: senior astronomers and early-career researchers. Design
  for the lowest common denominator.
