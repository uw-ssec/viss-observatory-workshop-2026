## Requirements

- Broad audience (senior and early-career); assume the lowest common
  denominator and give everyone a shared "ground floor".
- About 3/4 guided hands-on, 1/4 bring-your-own exploration; notebooks read
  as teaching documents; occasional deliberate errors so people do not just
  click through.
- One or two clicks from login to a working notebook; no terminal steps for
  setup.
- Emphasize **verification**: agents generate, humans and skills verify.
  Reuse existing scientific libraries rather than letting agents write
  bespoke duplicates.
- Content must be distinct from Schmidt's other Day 1 "build your own agent"
  and Day 2 hackathon sessions.
- State goals and structure up front; avoid cramming (lessons from the
  Cross-VISS talk rehearsals).
